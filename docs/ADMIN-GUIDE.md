# ADS Global admin — quick guide

Sign in at **www.adsglobaltd.com/admin/login** with your password, or choose "Email me a link". Accounts are created by a super admin (Users → Invite).

## Answer a request for quotation (RFQ)

1. **Enquiries** shows open items (New, In progress, Quoted). New ones are bold and counted in the sidebar. Use the tabs for RFQs, contact messages and partner applications.
2. Open the enquiry (click its reference, e.g. `ADS-2026-0014`).
3. In **Handling**: set **Assignee** to yourself and **Status** to *In progress*, then **Save changes**. The office was chosen automatically from the destination country; change it to hand over to the other office.
4. Download any **attachments** (links work for 5 minutes; reopen the page for a fresh one).
5. Click **Reply by email** — it opens your mail app addressed to the customer with the reference in the subject. Always keep the reference in the subject line.
6. Add **internal notes** for colleagues (supplier contacted, price received, etc.). Customers never see these.
7. When you've sent prices, set **Quoted**. Finish with **Won**, **Lost** or **Closed**.
8. **Export CSV** (top right of the inbox) downloads whatever the current filters show.

## Add or edit a product category

**Content → Products & services → Add category** (or click an existing one).
- **Summary** is the one line on the card; **Description** appears on the category page.
- **Example products** and **Sourcing regions**: one per line. Tick **Confirmed by ADS** once they're accurate — unconfirmed lists disappear when Claims mode is *Hide*.
- **Image**: click **Upload a photo** (landscape, about 2400 px wide) and write the **Alt text** (what the photo shows). Untick *This is a stock photo* for company photos.
- **Sort order**: lower numbers appear first. Untick **Published** to hide a category without deleting it.

## Change the numbers and claims (stats, ISO, 24/7…)

**Company settings → Claims & stats.**
- Edit a statistic's value, label or suffix (`+`, `%`) and tick **Confirmed** only when you have evidence. Write the source in the internal note.
- Claims (ISO status, 24/7 support, …) work the same way.
- **Claims mode**: *Flag* shows unconfirmed items with a dashed "Unconfirmed" marker (use while reviewing); *Hide* removes them from the public site (use when live).

## Other things you can change

- **Home hero** (Content → Home hero): headline lines, sub-headline, background photo.
- **Offices & hours, WhatsApp numbers, emails** (Settings → Offices). Phone numbers in international format without spaces: `+2349030428141`.
- **Which office gets which countries** (Settings → Contact & routing).
- **Social links, certifications, leadership** (Settings).
- **Projects** (Content → Projects): write, add a cover photo, tick **Published**.

Changes appear on the website within a few seconds. Every change is recorded in the **Audit log** with who made it and when (Lagos time).

## Users (super admins only)

**Users → Invite an admin**: enter email, name and role. If the email can't be sent, the page shows a one-time link to forward. Change a role or choose *No access* to remove someone. You can't change your own role.
